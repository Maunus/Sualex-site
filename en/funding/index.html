Funding

SUALEX is part of the project SUAHIST, "Exploring the Deep History of Southern Uto-Aztecan Languages and Peoples: A Mixed Methods Approach", funded by the European Research Council (ERC) through a Starting Grant (grant agreement 101116662, 2024–2029).

Funded by the European Union (ERC, SUAHIST, 101116662). Neither the European Union nor the granting authority can be held responsible for them.

Host institution

University of Copenhagen, Department of Cross-Cultural and Regional Studies. Principal investigator: Magnus Pharao Hansen.

Collaborators

Cenzontle A.C., a civil association created by the project and run by Edisa Altamirano Domínguez, Tutupika Carrillo de la Cruz, Vanessa Miranda Juárez and Rodrigo Parra Gutiérrez.

Acknowledgements

To the communities and speakers who shared their language, among them the Community of Xatsitsarie, La Yesca, Nayarit, and the Secundaria Tei Mɨayema. To Alan and Pamela Sandstrom and James Taggart for donating their texts to SUAText.
