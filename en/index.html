SUALEX

SUALEX brings together the language resources of the SUAHIST project, which studies the history of the Southern Uto-Aztecan peoples and languages of Mexico through language documentation and comparative historical linguistics, working with speakers, Indigenous scholars and communities.

The resources are published here as they become ready, with their open data in CLDF format.
