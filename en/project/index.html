The project

SUALEX and SUAHIST

SUALEX brings together the language resources of the SUAHIST project, "Exploring the Deep History of Southern Uto-Aztecan Languages and Peoples: A Mixed Methods Approach". The project studies the history of the Southern Uto-Aztecan peoples and languages of Mexico through language documentation and comparative historical linguistics, working with speakers, Indigenous scholars and communities.

The project is funded by the European Research Council (ERC) and based at the University of Copenhagen, from 2024 to 2029. The principal investigator is Magnus Pharao Hansen.

Open data

The data behind each resource will be published in CLDF (Cross-Linguistic Data Formats) and deposited in an archive with a DOI, so that it can be cited and reused.

Resources

  • WixaLex
    Dictionaries of the Wixárika (Huichol) language.
  • NayaLex
    Multidialect dictionary of Naayeri (Cora).
  • NawaLex
    Dictionaries of Nahua varieties, with a dialect database and map.
  • SUAEtym
    Southern Uto-Aztecan etymological dictionary.
  • SUAText
    Glossed texts in Uto-Aztecan languages.

None of the resources is published yet. They will be added here one at a time.
